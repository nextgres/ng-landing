Ask why any recommendation was made and get a real answer. No black box. No guessing. The explainability your team needs to own every decision — and defend it to anyone who asks.

Give your team a defensible answer for every recommendation, in plain language.

## How It Works

NEXTGRES provides full attribution for every recommendation. See which behavioral signals contributed, how much weight each signal carried, and how the recommendation would change if conditions were different.

## Key Benefits

- **Full decision transparency.** Every recommendation comes with a clear explanation.
- **Regulatory compliance.** Meet explainability requirements for regulated industries.
- **Team confidence.** Product, marketing, and leadership can all understand and trust the system.
