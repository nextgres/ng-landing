New users shouldn't get generic experiences just because you don't know them yet. NEXTGRES analyzes behavioral patterns from your existing user base and builds synthetic personas for brand-new users — so their very first experience is relevant, not a coin flip.

Teams report meaningful lift in Day 1 activation without touching onboarding flows.

## How It Works

NEXTGRES clusters your existing users by behavioral similarity, then matches new users to the most relevant cluster based on early signals: signup source, device, time of day, and initial actions. The result is a warm-start profile that drives personalization from session one.

## Key Benefits

- **Eliminate cold-start problems.** No more generic onboarding for new users.
- **Improve first-session engagement.** Relevant content and recommendations from the start.
- **Learn faster.** Synthetic profiles refine into real profiles as behavior accumulates.
