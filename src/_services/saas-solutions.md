A new user signs up from a high-intent campaign — and lands on the same generic onboarding flow as everyone else. They click around for three minutes, miss the feature that would have made them stay, and never come back. The behavioral signal that could have guided them sits in an event stream your product doesn't read until a batch job runs overnight.

NEXTGRES acts on in-app behavior the moment it happens. A user hesitates, skips a step, or explores a power feature — the experience adapts mid-session, not after a nightly retrain.

## Why SaaS Teams Choose NEXTGRES

- **Activate new users from their first session.** NEXTGRES detects onboarding friction in real time and surfaces the right feature, tooltip, or workflow before the user drops off. Day 1 activation stops being a coin flip.

- **Detect churn signals before they become churn.** A drop in login frequency, a skipped workflow, a change in usage pattern — NEXTGRES sees the signal and triggers re-engagement while the user is still reachable. Not after a weekly batch report flags them as "at risk."

- **No new pipelines. No ML team. No engineering dependency.** NEXTGRES connects read-only to your existing databases and event streams. Your product team controls personalization directly — define audiences, preview experiences, and understand every recommendation without filing a ticket.

## How It Works

### 1. Connect to Your Existing Stack

NEXTGRES connects read-only to your user profiles, feature usage logs, subscription data, and event streams — regardless of your language, framework, or database. No schema migration. No warehouse. No rebuild.

### 2. Detect Engagement Signals in Real Time

Logins, feature clicks, workflow completions, plan changes, support tickets, session depth — NEXTGRES detects these signals mid-session. The model updates as the user works, not after a pipeline runs overnight.

### 3. Personalize Every Touchpoint Instantly

In-app messages, onboarding flows, feature discovery, upgrade prompts, dashboard layouts — all driven by what the user just did, not what their segment did last quarter. Your product team previews every experience before it ships and can explain why any recommendation was made.

## Your Competitors Batch-Process Usage Data. You'll Personalize Mid-Session.

Traditional SaaS personalization means exporting events to a warehouse, waiting for a data team to build segments, and hoping engineering has bandwidth to act on them.
