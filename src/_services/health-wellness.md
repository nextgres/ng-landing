Most health personalization platforms need weeks of behavioral data before they can act — and when they do, the intervention arrives hours or days after the moment that mattered. A user skips a workout, abandons a meal log, or drops off mid-session. By the time a batch process surfaces that signal, the engagement window is closed.

NEXTGRES acts on behavioral signals the moment they happen. Not after a nightly batch. Not after a pipeline rebuild. In the same session.

## Why Health & Wellness Teams Choose NEXTGRES

- **Intervene when it matters, not hours later.** A skipped workout, a missed meal log, a drop in session frequency — NEXTGRES sees the signal and acts on it in real time, while the user is still reachable.

- **Sustained engagement without a data science team.** Define audiences in natural language, preview interventions before they ship, and understand why every recommendation was made — without filing a single engineering ticket.

- **No new pipelines. No new infrastructure.** NEXTGRES connects read-only to your existing databases and event streams. It determines which behavioral signals matter and ingests only what's needed. Your source systems are never modified.

## How It Works

### 1. Connect to Your Existing Data

NEXTGRES connects read-only to your user profiles, activity logs, wearable streams, and health data — regardless of how your data is structured. No rebuild. No warehouse. No schema migration.

### 2. Detect Behavioral Signals in Real Time

As users log meals, skip workouts, complete meditations, change preferences, or show signs of drop-off, NEXTGRES detects the behavioral shift mid-session. Not after a batch job runs overnight — while the user is still engaged.

### 3. Deliver Personalized Interventions Instantly

Trigger adaptive recommendations, timed nudges, or tailored content based on what each user just did — not what they did last week. NEXTGRES supports everything from daily habit reinforcement to long-term coaching journeys, all controlled by your product team directly.

## Enterprise Platforms Take Six Months. You'll Be Live in Hours.

Traditional health personalization vendors require dedicated ML teams, months of integration, and six-figure contracts before you see a single result. NEXTGRES connects to your existing data and delivers personalized recommendations the same day. Your product team controls it. No analyst in the loop.
