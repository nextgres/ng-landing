A customer logs in, checks their balance, uses the loan calculator twice, and leaves. The behavioral signal that could have surfaced the right product — a refinance offer, a credit line increase, a savings goal — sits in an event stream your personalization layer won't process until a batch job runs overnight. By then, they've opened a competitor's app.

NEXTGRES acts on financial behavior the moment it happens. A customer researches a product, changes their spending pattern, or shows early signs of attrition — the experience adapts mid-session, not after a weekly model retrain.

## Why Financial Services Teams Choose NEXTGRES

- **Surface the right product when intent is highest.** A customer uses the mortgage calculator, compares card rewards, or researches investment options — NEXTGRES detects the signal and presents the relevant offer before the session ends. Not after a batch process flags them as "interested" next week.

- **Detect attrition signals before they become closed accounts.** A drop in login frequency, a reduced transaction volume, a shift in direct deposit patterns — NEXTGRES sees the signal and triggers retention outreach while the relationship is still recoverable.

- **No new pipelines. No ML team. No engineering dependency.** NEXTGRES connects read-only to your existing core systems, transaction data, and event streams. Your product team controls personalization directly — define audiences, preview experiences, and understand every recommendation without filing a ticket.

## How It Works

### 1. Connect to Your Existing Systems

NEXTGRES connects read-only to your customer profiles, transaction history, product usage data, and event streams — whether you run on a modern cloud core, a legacy banking platform, or a hybrid stack. No re-architecture. No warehouse. No rebuild.

### 2. Detect Financial Behavior Signals in Real Time

Logins, balance checks, calculator usage, product page visits, transaction pattern changes, support interactions — NEXTGRES detects these signals mid-session. The model updates as the customer engages, not after a pipeline runs overnight.

### 3. Personalize Every Touchpoint Instantly

Product offers, homepage banners, in-app nudges, advisory prompts, notification timing — all driven by what the customer just did, not what their segment did last quarter. Your product team previews every experience before it ships and can explain why any recommendation was made.

## Your Competitors Batch-Process Transactions. You'll Personalize Mid-Session.

Traditional financial personalization means exporting transaction data to a warehouse, waiting for a data team to build segments, and hoping engineering has bandwidth to act on them. NEXTGRES connects to your existing data and delivers personalized experiences the same day. Your product team controls it. No analyst in the loop.
